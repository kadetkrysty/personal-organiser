    Settings

    Reminders

    Tasks synced to Google Calendar are reminded by the Calendar app, even when this app is closed. Other tasks are reminded by this app while it is open.

    Sync with Google

    Keeps your tasks the same on all your devices (stored in a hidden app folder in your Google Drive) and adds them to Google Calendar. Use the same Google account and Client ID on every device.

    How to get your Client ID

    You only do this once (about 10 minutes, free). It gives this app permission to use your Google account. Easiest on a computer.

    1. Open Google Cloud Console and sign in with your Google account. Create a new project called Personal Organiser, then click Create.
    2. Turn on the two services this app uses. Open each link, check your new project is selected at the top, and click Enable: Google Calendar API and Google Drive API.
    3. Open the sign-in setup page and click Get started. App name: Personal Organiser, support email: your email. Choose External, enter your email again, agree and click Create.
    4. Go to Audience, under Test users click Add users and add your own Google email.
    5. Go to Clients and click Create client. Application type: Web application.
    6. Under Authorised JavaScript origins click Add URI and paste this app's address: If you also use the app from another address (for example your phone's web address), add that too. Then click Create.
    7. Copy the Client ID shown (it ends in .apps.googleusercontent.com) and paste it in the box below.
    8. Click Connect Google account. Google will say the app is "unverified" - that is normal for your own personal app: click Continue and tick both permissions.

    Use the same Client ID on all your devices. You can find it again any time on the Clients page.

    New to this? Tap the i next to "Google OAuth Client ID" for step-by-step help.

    Backup

    Export a copy of your lists and tasks for safekeeping. Google sync can also keep your devices up to date. Importing a backup replaces your current lists and tasks.
